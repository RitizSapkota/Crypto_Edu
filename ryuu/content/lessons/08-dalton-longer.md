### Value area placement

Compare today's value area to yesterday's.

| Placement | Reading |
| --- | --- |
| Clearly higher or lower | Value is migrating. The trend is real. |
| Overlapping to higher or lower | A trend that is slowing, or a balance that is starting to lean. |
| Unchanged or inside | Balance. Energy building for the next move. |
| Outside (wider on both sides) | Both sides active. Unstable balance. |

Price making a new high while value stays overlapping is a divergence. Price went, value did not follow.

### One-timeframing

A market is one-timeframing up when each bar's low is above the previous bar's low, on whatever bar size you choose: 30 minutes, a day, a week. It means one side is in control on that timeframe. The first bar that breaks the sequence ends it. That does not mean reversal. It means the easy part is over.

### The balance rules

When a market has built a multi-day balance area, there are three outcomes, and each has a plan:

1. **It stays inside.** Fade the edges back toward the middle.
2. **It looks outside and fails.** Price pokes beyond an edge, finds no acceptance and comes back in. The target is the opposite edge.
3. **It breaks out and is accepted.** Value builds outside. Go with it.

The second one is the same pattern as your sweep and reclaim.

### Inventory

Short-term traders can get too long or too short. When they do, the market has to correct that before anything else can happen.

- **Short covering.** Shorts buy back, price rallies fast, then stalls. The profile is a P: a thin stem and a fat top.
- **Long liquidation.** Longs sell out, price drops fast, then stalls. The profile is a b: a thin stem and a fat bottom.

Both are old business being closed, not new business being opened. They run out. This is the Market Profile version of what you read from open interest: on Nov 10 the rally came on falling OI, which is short covering.

### Poor structure gets repaired

Profiles built in a hurry leave defects: poor highs and lows, thin stretches, single prints, a POC that sits far from the close. Dalton's view is that the market tends to come back and repair these. The see gap, fill gap rule from the profile lessons is the same idea.

### Trade location

The same idea is a good trade at the edge of value and a poor one in the middle. At the edge, the level that proves you wrong is close and the target is far. In the middle it is the reverse.

### Check yourself

<details>
<summary>Price rallies 2% in an hour. The profile is a P shape and open interest fell throughout. What kind of rally is it?</summary>

Short covering. Old shorts are closing, no new longs are arriving. Expect it to stall once the covering is done, unless new initiative buying appears above.

</details>

<details>
<summary>A five-day balance. Price trades 0.5% above the top for twenty minutes and falls back inside. Plan?</summary>

That is the look above and fail. The framework's target is the opposite side of the balance, with the failed high as the level that proves it wrong.

</details>
