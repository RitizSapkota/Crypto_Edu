### Where the open sits

| Open location | State | Opportunity and risk |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Inside prior value | Balance. The market agrees with yesterday. | Lowest. Expect rotation unless something drives out. |
| Outside value, inside prior range | Mild imbalance. | Moderate. Watch for a return into value. |
| Outside prior range | Imbalance. Something changed overnight. | Highest. A drive away is initiative. A failure back into range is a strong reversal signal. |

### The four opening types

| Type | What it looks like | Conviction | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Open-Drive** | Opens and goes one way without returning to the open. | Highest | Do not fade. Join on a shallow pullback. |
| **Open-Test-Drive** | Probes one way to a known level, finds nothing, reverses and drives the other way. | High | Go with the drive. The failed test extreme is your stop reference. |
| **Open-Rejection-Reverse** | Opens, extends one way, meets opposing activity and reverses back through the open. | Lower | Trade back toward prior value with smaller size. The early extreme holds only some of the time. |
| **Open-Auction** | Rotates around the open with no commitment. | Lowest | Wait for the initial balance to finish. Then trade an accepted extension, or fade the edges if balance holds. |

### The 80% rule

If price opens outside the prior value area, comes back in, and holds inside for two consecutive 30-minute periods, the rule says it has a high chance of travelling to the other side of the value area. The POC is the usual first target.

> **How much to trust it.**
>
> The 80% figure is trader lore with no published study behind it. One trader's test reported on NexusFi found the strict textbook version only 8 times in about 1,000 S&P futures sessions, and it worked 5 times. A separate study of 455 S&P sessions found no standalone edge from opening types or day types either: open auctions inside range produced trend days about as often as open drives did (17.3% against 16.4%). Use the open types and the 80% rule to organise what you see in the first 30 to 60 minutes. Do not treat them as signals.

### Crypto has no opening bell

Bitcoin trades all week, so there is no single open. The practical substitutes are the session opens, the daily open and the weekly open. Lesson "Sessions and kill zones" covers that.
