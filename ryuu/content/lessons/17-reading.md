### Books

- *Mind Over Markets*, James Dalton, Eric Jones and Robert Dalton. Day structure, day types, opening types, initiative and responsive activity.
- *Markets in Profile*, James Dalton, Robert Dalton and Eric Jones. Timeframes, inventory, balance rules, context.

The Dalton lessons in this course are our own summary of the framework, checked against public explainers. They are a map of the books, not a replacement. Read the books for the examples and the detail.
